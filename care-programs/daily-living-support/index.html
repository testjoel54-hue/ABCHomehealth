CARE PROGRAMS / SUPPORT FOR DAILY LIVING

Familiar routines.
Thoughtful support.

A personal approach to the activities that shape everyday life at home.

A PERSONAL STARTING POINT

The details of daily life belong in the conversation.

Personal care routines and household activities can be important parts of feeling comfortable at home. Understanding where help is needed gives the care team a clearer picture of your daily experience.

THOUGHTFUL PLANNING

Make room for
what matters.

01 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Talk through the day

Share which routines are comfortable and where you need assistance.

02 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Respect preferences

Discuss personal choices, privacy, and the way you prefer tasks to be approached.

03 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Confirm the scope

Ask exactly which tasks are included, who will assist, and when visits are scheduled.

ASK YOUR CARE TEAM

Good questions.
A shared understanding.

  • Which daily activities can be addressed in my plan?
  • Which tasks are outside the service provided?
  • How should we prepare for scheduled visits?
