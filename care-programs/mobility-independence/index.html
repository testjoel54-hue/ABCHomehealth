CARE PROGRAMS / MOBILITY & INDEPENDENCE

Everyday movement.
Personal milestones.

Conversations and care planning around the way you move through your own home.

A PERSONAL STARTING POINT

Start with the activities that matter to you.

Getting out of a chair, moving between rooms, or taking part in a familiar routine may be meaningful priorities. Therapy goals should reflect your abilities, your home environment, and the activities you value.

THOUGHTFUL PLANNING

01 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Describe your routines

03 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Include chosen caregivers

Discuss the type of help recommended and what caregivers should learn from the team.

ASK YOUR CARE TEAM

Good questions.
A shared understanding.

  • Which mobility goals are appropriate for my plan?
  • What should my caregiver understand about assistance?
  • How will we review progress and changing needs?
