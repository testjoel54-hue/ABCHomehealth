CARE PROGRAMS / RECOVERING AT HOME

A familiar place.
A supported transition.

A starting point for planning support after a hospital stay or a change in health needs.

A PERSONAL STARTING POINT

The next chapter deserves a clear plan.

Coming home can mean new instructions, different routines, and plenty of questions. A coordinated plan helps you understand what has been recommended and what to discuss with the people involved in your care.

THOUGHTFUL PLANNING

Make room for
what matters.

01 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Know the next steps

Review who is involved, which visits are planned, and how follow-up questions will be handled.

02 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Gather the essentials

Keep discharge instructions, a current medication list, and healthcare-provider details together.

03 / PLANNING TOGETHER

Share what matters

Discuss everyday challenges, household routines, and the goals you want the team to understand.

ASK YOUR CARE TEAM

Good questions.
A shared understanding.

  • What information should be reviewed before the first visit?
  • Who should we contact about changes to the plan?
  • How will visits and follow-up appointments fit together?
