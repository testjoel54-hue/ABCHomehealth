PATIENTS & FAMILIES

A little guidance.
A clearer path.

Helpful information as you explore care at home for yourself or someone you love.

YOUR FIRST STEPS

Start with
a conversation.

01 / START THE CONVERSATION

Tell us what’s next.

Discuss your care needs, a recent discharge, or questions about support at home. A family member or healthcare professional can help start the conversation.

02 / REVIEW THE DETAILS

Understand the options.

The agency reviews service availability, referral requirements, coverage, and whether your needs can be supported at home.

03 / PLAN TOGETHER

Take the next step.

When appropriate, an assessment helps guide a plan of care. Ask about visit timing, your care team, and how your family can be involved.

BEFORE THE FIRST VISIT

A few essentials.
A smoother start.

  • A current medication list
  • Relevant discharge and care instructions
  • Insurance and healthcare-provider information
  • Questions, priorities, and personal preferences
  • Contact details for chosen caregivers

The agency will explain what else is needed and how to share information through its approved process.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR CARE

Know the plan.
Ask what matters.

01 / VISIT EXPECTATIONS

Who will visit?

Ask which care professionals will visit, what each visit may involve, and how scheduling changes are communicated.

02 / COVERAGE & COSTS

What should I verify?

Ask about benefits, authorizations, agency participation, and any expected costs before care begins. Coverage is never assumed.

03 / CHANGING NEEDS

Who should I contact?

Ask how to reach the care team with questions and what to do if symptoms or support needs change. Follow your individual care instructions.

FAMILY INVOLVEMENT

Part of the
conversation.

Caregivers can bring useful observations and questions. Let the agency know whom you want included.

Can my family be involved?+

Ask how your chosen family members or caregivers can participate in planning and communication. Your preferences and permission should guide their involvement.

Does someone stay in my home all day?+

Home health commonly involves scheduled visits. Continuous supervision, overnight care, and round-the-clock personal care should not be assumed to be included. Confirm the visit schedule and scope with the agency.

What happens if my needs change?+

Tell your care team about changes and ask how the care plan will be reviewed. For urgent concerns, follow the instructions provided by your care team. In a medical emergency, call 911.

UNDERSTAND THE EXPERIENCE

From first questions
to the next chapter.

  1. Discuss your needs

    Share priorities, location, and the type of support being considered.

  2. Review the details

    Confirm availability, referral information, benefits, and expected costs.

  3. Understand the plan

    Ask about goals, visit disciplines, timing, and how to reach the care team.

  4. Stay in the conversation

    Share changes and review next steps with the people involved in your care.
