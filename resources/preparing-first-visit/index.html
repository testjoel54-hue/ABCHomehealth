PATIENT & FAMILY GUIDE

A few essentials.
A smoother start.

A practical checklist to help you get ready for a home health visit.

01

Keep key information together

Have your current medication list, relevant care instructions, healthcare-provider information, and coverage details available. Ask the agency which records it needs and how to share them.

02

Make space for a conversation

Choose a place where you can talk comfortably. Let the agency know about access needs, communication preferences, or other arrangements that would help the visit.

03

Write down your priorities

List the questions you want answered and the daily activities or routines you would like to discuss. A short list can help keep the conversation focused.

04

Know what happens next

Before the visit ends, ask whom to contact with questions, what follow-up is planned, and how visit timing will be communicated.

General planning information. Follow the individual instructions provided by your healthcare team.
