PATIENT & FAMILY GUIDE

Good questions.
A clearer picture.

Use these prompts to prepare for a care-planning conversation.

01

About the plan

What goals are we working toward? Which disciplines are involved? How will we review the plan if my needs change?

02

About visits

Who will visit, what will each visit focus on, and how will scheduling changes be shared? Who should we contact with a scheduling question?

03

About instructions

Can we review the instructions together? Which recommendations should be clarified by my healthcare provider? What should chosen caregivers understand?

04

About the next step

What follow-up is planned? How will we know when a change or transition in care is being considered? What information should we keep available?

General planning information. Follow the individual instructions provided by your healthcare team.
