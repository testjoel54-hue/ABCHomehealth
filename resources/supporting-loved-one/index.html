PATIENT & FAMILY GUIDE

A helping hand.
A listening ear.

A starting point for family members and chosen caregivers.

01

Begin with preferences

Ask how your loved one wants to be involved and whom they want included. Their priorities should guide conversations about support.

02

Listen for everyday concerns

Make room for questions about routines, comfort, and practical challenges. Bring observations to the care team without assuming you need to solve every concern yourself.

03

Clarify the support plan

Understand which tasks the agency provides and where other help may be needed. Ask the team to explain recommended caregiver involvement.

04

Ask about resources

Discuss planning questions and available community supports with the care team. Additional resources may have their own availability and eligibility requirements.

General planning information. Follow the individual instructions provided by your healthcare team.
