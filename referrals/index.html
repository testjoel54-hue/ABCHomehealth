REFERRAL OVERVIEW

The right details.
A clearer handoff.

01 / CONFIRM AVAILABILITY

Start with the basics.

Confirm the patient’s location, requested disciplines, service availability, and agency requirements before sharing records.

02 / USE THE APPROVED PROCESS

Share information securely.

Ask the agency for its approved referral channel and the records required for review. Referral contact details are pending.

03 / COORDINATE NEXT STEPS

Keep expectations clear.

Acceptance, timing, coverage, and the plan for next steps must be confirmed by the agency. Sending a referral does not confirm admission.

PREPARING A REFERRAL

Information to
have ready.

The agency will specify the information it needs. Common categories to discuss include:

  • Patient location and preferred contact details
  • Referring provider and follow-up contact
  • Requested disciplines and care needs
  • Relevant orders and discharge information
  • Coverage and authorization information
  • Timing, caregiver availability, and transition concerns

A SHARED UNDERSTANDING

Before the handoff.
Confirm the details.

  • Confirm service area and discipline availability.
  • Clarify the information required for agency review.
  • Use the agency’s approved channel for records.
  • Confirm acceptance and timing directly.
  • Share the next steps with the patient and chosen caregivers.
