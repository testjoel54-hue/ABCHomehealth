FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

A starting point for patients, families, and the professionals who support them.

What is home health care?+

Home health brings certain healthcare services into the home. The type of care, visit schedule, and length of service depend on individual needs and the plan of care.

How do I start the process?+

Begin by discussing your needs with the agency and your healthcare provider. Ask about the service area, availability, referral requirements, and the information needed for a review.

Which services are available?+

The services shown on this website are a sample home health offering. Confirm the agency’s final services, staffing availability, and service area before arranging care.

Will my insurance cover care?+

Coverage depends on your plan, eligibility, services ordered, and agency participation. Ask the agency and your insurer to verify benefits, authorization requirements, and expected costs.

Does someone stay in my home all day?+

Home health commonly involves scheduled visits. Continuous supervision, overnight care, and round-the-clock personal care should not be assumed to be included. Confirm the visit schedule and scope with the agency.

Can my family be involved?+

Ask how your chosen family members or caregivers can participate in planning and communication. Your preferences and permission should guide their involvement.

What should I prepare for the first visit?+

Have a current medication list, relevant discharge or care instructions, insurance information, and questions ready. The agency can explain what else is needed.

What happens if my needs change?+

Tell your care team about changes and ask how the care plan will be reviewed. For urgent concerns, follow the instructions provided by your care team. In a medical emergency, call 911.

How do referral partners contact the agency?+

Referral contact details and the approved process will be added before launch. Do not send patient records through an unverified email address or a general website form.

Is this website a patient portal?+

No. This website provides general information and does not collect patient records or provide a patient portal.
