GETTING STARTED

A little guidance.
A clearer path.

Start with your questions. Take it one step at a time.

01 / START THE CONVERSATION

Tell us what’s next.

Discuss your care needs, a recent discharge, or questions about support at home. A family member or healthcare professional can help start the conversation.

02 / REVIEW THE DETAILS

Understand the options.

The agency reviews service availability, referral requirements, coverage, and whether your needs can be supported at home.

03 / PLAN TOGETHER

Take the next step.

When appropriate, an assessment helps guide a plan of care. Ask about visit timing, your care team, and how your family can be involved.

What is home health care?+

Home health brings certain healthcare services into the home. The type of care, visit schedule, and length of service depend on individual needs and the plan of care.

How do I start the process?+

Begin by discussing your needs with the agency and your healthcare provider. Ask about the service area, availability, referral requirements, and the information needed for a review.

Which services are available?+

The services shown on this website are a sample home health offering. Confirm the agency’s final services, staffing availability, and service area before arranging care.

Will my insurance cover care?+

Coverage depends on your plan, eligibility, services ordered, and agency participation. Ask the agency and your insurer to verify benefits, authorization requirements, and expected costs.
