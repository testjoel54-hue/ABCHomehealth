COVERAGE & PAYMENT

Understand the details.
Plan with clarity.

Benefits and costs are individual. Start by asking the agency and your insurer to explain the details that apply to you.

BEFORE CARE BEGINS

A few questions.
Clearer expectations.

01 / PLAN PARTICIPATION

Is the agency in my network?

Confirm whether the agency participates with your specific plan. A plan name alone does not establish coverage.

02 / ELIGIBILITY & APPROVAL

What needs to be reviewed?

Ask about eligibility, required orders, authorization, and documentation. These depend on the plan and services involved.

03 / EXPECTED COSTS

What might I owe?

Ask your insurer and the agency to explain applicable patient costs and how changes will be communicated.

TERMS YOU MAY HEAR

A clearer
conversation.

Network

The providers or agencies that participate in a particular insurance plan.

Authorization

A review or approval a plan may require for certain services. Ask how it applies to your care.

Deductible

An amount you may need to pay before certain plan benefits apply, depending on your coverage.

Copayment or coinsurance

A fixed amount or share of a covered cost that may be your responsibility under your plan.
