PHYSICAL THERAPY AT HOME

Working toward the moments that matter.

Moving between rooms, getting out of a chair, or returning to a familiar routine can be meaningful goals. Physical therapy considers your mobility needs and the way you move through your home.

Strength and movement

Practice activities selected for your needs and abilities.

Balance and coordination

Work on exercises and strategies tailored to your mobility goals.

Walking and transfers

Address movement between surfaces and walking routines as appropriate.

Home exercise education

Understand the activities prescribed by your therapist and how to practice them.
