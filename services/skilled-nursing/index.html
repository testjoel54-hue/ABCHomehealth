SKILLED NURSING AT HOME

A clearer understanding of your health.

A nursing visit is an opportunity to talk through changes, review your care plan, and build confidence in the routines that support your health. Your needs and the orders in your plan guide the care provided.

Health assessments

Review symptoms, monitor changes, and discuss concerns with your nurse.

Medication education

Understand the medications in your plan and the questions to raise with your healthcare provider.

Wound care support

Receive ordered wound care and education about your individual care instructions.

Condition education

Learn about care routines and when to contact your healthcare team.
