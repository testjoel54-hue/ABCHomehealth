OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY AT HOME

Care that fits your daily life.

Occupational therapy focuses on the daily activities that matter to you. Your therapist may explore how your abilities, routines, and home environment can work together to support participation.

Personal routines

Explore strategies for dressing, bathing, and other daily activities.

Home safety

Review everyday tasks and identify concerns to discuss with your care team.

Adaptive approaches

Learn about techniques or equipment that may support your goals.

Caregiver education

Help chosen caregivers understand recommended approaches and support needs.
